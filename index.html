DSP Suite
A note from the developer

A chapter completed.

DSP Suite has been retired and is no longer being actively developed, sold, or supported.

Created by a Direct Support Professional, DSP Suite began as an effort to make everyday work in residential care a little easier. The project explored private, offline tools for documentation, resident support, and staff workflows.

Thank you to everyone who took an interest in the project. The experience and ideas behind DSP Suite continue to inform my work, but my focus has now shifted to a different caregiving resource.

What's next

Meet My Care Journals

My Care Journals is a separate, privacy-focused app created for family caregivers, with practical learning resources and optional journaling tools. Like DSP Suite, it reflects a commitment to useful, accessible software that respects people's privacy.
