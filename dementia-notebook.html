The Origin

Why This App Exists

Dementia runs big on my mother's side of the family. Everybody gets it.

When a family member is suddenly thrust into the caregiving seat with zero training and no idea what to do next, the feeling of being overwhelmed is immediate.

I built this app to bridge that exact gap. It's designed to give you practical, calm guidance right when you need it most during a difficult moment.

"No ads, no subscriptions, no cloud tracking. Just a reliable tool built to help families navigate the hardest moments with confidence." — Creator of the Dementia Notebook
