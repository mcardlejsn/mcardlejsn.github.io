An anchor in a chaotic time.

When you are managing the health and safety of a loved one, you don't need "features"—you need clarity. The Dementia Notebook is designed to capture observations quickly, so you can focus on the person, not the software.

Key Caregiver Features

  • 100% Offline: No account required. Your data never touches a server.
  • Structured Coaching: Built-in de-escalation tips for Confusion, Agitation, and Sundowning.
  • Secure Journaling: Protect sensitive care logs with your phone's fingerprint or face scan.
  • Safety Checklists: Pre-loaded guides to help you identify home hazards before they become incidents.
